Our roots

The farm behind the company.

The Adela Rose began as a small farm in Caledonia, Michigan: Belted Galloway cattle, horses, English shepherds and a tractor bought at an online auction. The farm name stuck, and the habits came with it: make things work better, and stay practical.

Meet Thor, our farm herd sire and the company mascot. He had rare imported bloodlines and a calm, steady temperament, which is the kind of company we want to be.
